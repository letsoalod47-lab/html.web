Frequently Asked Questions

What plumbing services do you provide?

We provide leak detection and pipe repairs, drain cleaning, geyser work, bathroom and kitchen installations, emergency plumbing, and water pressure management.

Do you handle plumbing emergencies?

Yes. Emergency service is available for burst pipes, major leaks, and blocked sewers, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve homes and businesses across Limpopo, including Polokwane, Seshego, and Tzaneen.

Can you install or repair a geyser?

Yes. We install, repair, and maintain electric and solar geysers, including elements, thermostats, and burst geyser replacements.

How do I request a plumbing service?

Send your request through our service enquiry form, and include details about the plumbing issue.
