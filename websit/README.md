## Project Overview
The Masepa Industry website is a professional multi-page business website created to promote a plumbing and maintenance service company operating in Limpopo, South Africa. The project was developed using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript to create a clean, responsive and user-friendly digital presence for the business.

The website provides an online introduction to the company, showcases its services, presents key business information, and allows visitors to submit enquiries and contact the business. The overall design is structured to build trust, communicate reliability, and support customer engagement.

## Website Goals and Objectives
The main goals of the website are to:
- Present The Masepa Industry as a professional and trustworthy plumbing service provider.
- Provide clear information about the company, its experience, and its values.
- Showcase the range of plumbing products and services offered to residential and commercial clients.
- Make it easier for customers to request services or ask enquiries online.
- Improve the company’s digital presence and accessibility on desktop and mobile devices.
- Create a consistent and visually appealing website that reflects the brand identity.

## Key Features and Functionality
The website includes the following key features:
- Responsive navigation menu with a mobile-friendly toggle.
- Professional homepage with branding, tagline, and call-to-action button.
- About Us page with company history, mission, vision, and leadership profiles.
- Services page with six service categories and clear promotional information.
- Enquiry form for potential customers to request services or ask questions.
- Contact page with office details, business information, and contact form.
- Consistent styling across all pages using a shared stylesheet.
- Lightweight static website structure suitable for simple hosting and future expansion.

## Timeline and Milestones
The project was planned and implemented in stages to support structured development and progressive testing.

- Initial research and planning: Business goals, target audience, and required website sections.
- Content and design preparation: Company information, service descriptions, branding, and layout planning.
- Homepage and navigation development: Creation of the main landing page and menu structure.
- About and Services pages: Company profile and service catalogue sections completed.
- Enquiry and Contact pages: Forms and business contact information added.
- Testing and refinement: Responsiveness checks, layout consistency, and final documentation.
- Final submission: README documentation and project packaging prepared.
