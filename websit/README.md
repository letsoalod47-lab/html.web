## Project Overview
The Masepa Industry website is a professional multi-page business website created to promote a plumbing and maintenance service company operating in Limpopo, South Africa. The project was developed using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript to create a clean, responsive and user-friendly digital presence for the business.

The website provides an online introduction to the company, showcases its services, presents key business information, and allows visitors to submit enquiries and contact the business. The overall design is structured to build trust, communicate reliability, and support customer engagement.

## Website Goals and Objectives
The main goals of the website are to:
- Present The Masepa Industry as a professional and trustworthy plumbing service provider.
- Provide clear information about the company, its experience, and its values.
- Showcase the range of plumbing products and services offered to residential and commercial clients.
- Make it easier for customers to request services or ask enquiries online.
- Improve the company’s digital presence and accessibility on desktop and mobile devices.
- Create a consistent and visually appealing website that reflects the brand identity.

## Key Features and Functionality
The website includes the following key features:
- Responsive navigation menu with a mobile-friendly toggle.
- Professional homepage with branding, tagline, and call-to-action button.
- About Us page with company history, mission, vision, and leadership profiles.
- Services page with six service categories and clear promotional information.
- Enquiry form for potential customers to request services or ask questions. The form validates entries and opens a prefilled email draft; it does not yet submit directly from the website.
- Contact page with office details, business information, maps, and contact form.
- Local SEO with Polokwane-focused page titles and descriptions, consistent primary business contact details, LocalBusiness structured data, and a crawlable sitemap.
- Shared responsive footer with working YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook profile links.
- Green bottom-up hover animation for the social icons, with reduced-motion preferences respected.
- Project gallery on the About Us page with an accessible image lightbox.
- Dynamic Services page with JavaScript-rendered service cards, keyword search, sorting, category filters, and a live result count.
- Consistent styling across all pages using a shared stylesheet.
- Lightweight static website structure suitable for simple hosting and future expansion.

## Timeline and Milestones
The project was planned and implemented in stages to support structured development and progressive testing.

- Initial research and planning: Business goals, target audience, and required website sections.
- Content and design preparation: Company information, service descriptions, branding, and layout planning.
- Homepage and navigation development: Creation of the main landing page and menu structure.
- About and Services pages: Company profile and service catalogue sections completed.
- Enquiry and Contact pages: Forms and business contact information added.
- Testing and refinement: Responsiveness checks, layout consistency, and final documentation.
- Footer refinement: Social media icons and a bottom-up hover fill animation added consistently across all pages.
- Final submission: README documentation and project packaging prepared.

## Part 1 Details
Part 1 established the core plumbing business website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It introduced the main pages and navigation, company and leadership information, service descriptions, enquiry form, and contact information.

## Part 2 Details
Part 2 focused on the website's CSS styling. A shared stylesheet defines the color palette, typography, spacing, buttons, page layouts, service cards, contact sections, and visual details used across the pages. Responsive CSS rules adapt the navigation, grids, forms, and other content for desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes. Hover, focus, and reduced-motion styles help make interactions clear and comfortable to use.

## Part 3 Details
Part 3 adds JavaScript interactions and further improvements across the website:
- Mobile navigation opens and closes with its menu button, closes after a navigation link is selected, and supports the Escape key.
- Service search, sorting, and category filters update the rendered service cards and announce the number of results. FAQ items behave like an accordion, with only one answer open at a time.
- The About Us project gallery opens images in a lightbox that can be closed with its close button, by clicking the overlay, or with the Escape key.
- Enquiry forms use browser validation, prepare a prefilled email draft, and prevent selecting a preferred date in the past. Direct website submission requires a configured form-processing service endpoint.
- Selecting an enquiry service displays only the relevant follow-up questions.
- Footer links open the provided YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook profiles in a new tab. Their green hover animation fills upward from the bottom; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Local SEO metadata and structured business information target Polokwane. `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` point to the published Netlify site.

## Sitemap
The website structure follows a simple, user-friendly sitemap:

- Home

- Services

- Enquiry

  - Business contacts, locations, and contact form
